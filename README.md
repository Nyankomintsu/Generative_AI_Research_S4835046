1. Storage and Accessibility: Explain why it is important to store and organise your files efficiently.
Answers. Keeping files organised helps us find what we need and avoid using the wrong version. It also makes teamwork easier and lets others check our data and results.

2. Specify which data types may require special access controls. Explain your reasons. (2-3 sentences)
Answers. Signed consent forms, survey responses, interview transcripts and photos that could identify participants need restricted access. This protects their privacy and keeps personal information within the approved research team.

3. Why is it important to have a good naming convention?
Answers. Clear and consistent file names help us understand what each file contains and which version it is. They also make files easier to find and reduce the chance of using the wrong one.
